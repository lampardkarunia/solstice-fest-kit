Getting there

Free shuttles from Canggu, Ubud and the airport every 40 minutes from 13:00. Scooter parking on site; no cars past the village gate.

Stay over

The lawn campsite opens Friday evening — pitch included with a Sunset Duo. Partner guesthouses in Uluwatu from $25 a night.

Good to know

Cashless site, free water everywhere, quiet room by the Dune Stage, and a lit — but steep — cliff path. Wear real shoes.
